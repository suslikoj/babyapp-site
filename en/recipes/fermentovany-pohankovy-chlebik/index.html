Fermented buckwheat bread

A simple fermented bread made only from buckwheat, without flour or yeast. Thanks to natural fermentation, it is more digestible and supports the intestinal microbiome.

Prep
15 min
Cook
60 min
Total
75 min
Servings
12
Difficulty
medium

Allergen-freeVeganTravel friendly

Contains allergens: none of the tracked allergens

May contain: Seeds

Free from (by ingredients): dairy, egg, wheat, soy, nuts, peanuts, fish

Ingredients

for 12 servings

  • 500 g Buckwheat
  • 2 spoons Apple cider vinegar
  • 2 spoons Seeds – optional

Method

  1. Cover the buckwheat with water, add a tablespoon of apple cider vinegar and let it soak for at least 7 hours, ideally overnight.

  2. Then drain the buckwheat, rinse thoroughly and let it drain.

  3. Add a cup of water and salt to the buckwheat and blend into a smooth, thick mixture.

  4. Depending on your taste, you can add seeds and stir briefly or leave them completely seedless.

  5. Transfer the mixture to a container, cover, and let ferment at room temperature for approximately 24 hours.

  6. After fermentation, pour the dough into a bread pan lined with baking paper.

  7. Bake in a preheated oven at 210°C for approximately 50–60 minutes, until the surface is golden.

  8. After baking, let the bread cool completely, then slice and use as regular bread.

Tip

The bread is best when it has cooled completely. If you don't want to use it right away, you can slice it and freeze it.
