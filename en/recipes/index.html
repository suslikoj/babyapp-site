Broth

130 min

Properly prepared broth is one of the basic pillars of supporting digestion, the intestinal mucosa and the microbiome. Thanks to the content of minerals, amino acids, gelatin and collagen, it helps in healing leaky gut and can significantly support the treatment of eczema and allergies. However, the length of cooking and the right choice of ingredients are important, especially in cases of histamine overload.
