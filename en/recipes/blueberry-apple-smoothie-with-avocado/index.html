Blueberry-apple smoothie with avocado

A fruit smoothie with a delicate creamy texture thanks to avocado. Blueberries provide antioxidants (especially anthocyanins), apples provide fiber and natural sweetness. A quick snack or light breakfast with no added sugar.

Prep
5 min
Total
5 min
Servings
2
Difficulty
easy

Under 30 minNo cookTravel friendly

Contains allergens: Berries

Free from (by ingredients): dairy, egg, wheat, soy, nuts, peanuts, fish

Ingredients

for 2 servings

  • 1 pcs Apple
  • 2 handfuls Blueberries
  • 1 pcs Avocado
  • 0.5 cup Water

Method

  1. Wash the apple and remove the core.

  2. Halve the avocado, remove the pulp.

  3. Put everything in a blender, add water.

  4. Mix until smooth.

  5. Serve immediately.

Tip

For a thicker smoothie, add less water, for a lighter consistency, add more. For a colder version, use frozen blueberries.
