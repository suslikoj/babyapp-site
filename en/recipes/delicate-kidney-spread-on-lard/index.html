Delicate kidney spread on lard

A very delicate spread made from pork kidneys, onions and lard. Thanks to a short heat treatment and subsequent mixing, it has a smooth texture and full flavor. Kidneys are naturally rich in protein, B vitamins and minerals.

Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Total
25 min
Servings
6
Difficulty
easy

Allergen-freeUnder 30 minOne pot

Contains allergens: none of the tracked allergens

Free from (by ingredients): dairy, egg, wheat, soy, nuts, peanuts, fish

Ingredients

for 6 servings

  • 600 g Pork kidneys
  • 2 pcs Onion
  • 6 cloves Garlic
  • 4 spoons Lard

Method

  1. Clean the kidneys and cut them into small cubes.

  2. Heat about half of the lard in a pan and fry the kidneys on one side.

  3. Add the chopped onion, stir and sauté for a few more minutes until the onion softens.

  4. Add the chopped garlic and cook briefly until fragrant.

  5. Remove the mixture from the stove and transfer it to a blender.

  6. Add the rest of the lard and blend into a smooth spread.

  7. Salt to taste. If you want an even finer texture, you can add a small amount of lard.

Tip

If you don't like offal, you can make the spread first for your child or family. Thanks to its very mild flavor and creamy consistency, it's hard to tell that it's offal, and it often surprises even those who don't normally seek it out.
