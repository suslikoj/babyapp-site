Banana bread

A soft, egg-free banana bread that can be easily adapted for testing or training specific allergens. Great as a snack or breakfast.

Prep
15 min
Cook
60 min
Total
75 min
Servings
12
Difficulty
easy

Vegan

Contains allergens: Exotic fruits

May contain: Seeds

Free from (by ingredients): dairy, egg, wheat, soy, nuts, peanuts, fish

Ingredients

for 12 servings

  • 1.5 spoon Crushed flax seeds (nebo 0,5 banánu navíc) – optional
  • 5 pcs Bananas
  • 1 third (1/3) of a cup Melted fat (ghee, lard, coconut fat or butter)
  • 1 half (1/2) cup Water or plant-based milk
  • 1 half a teaspoon Salt
  • 1 half (1/2) cup Coconut flour
  • 1 half (1/2) cup Rice flour

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.

  2. Mix the crushed flax seeds with 4 tablespoons of water and let them swell for about 5 minutes.

  3. Mash 4 bananas in a bowl.

  4. Add the seeds, melted fat, liquid and salt.

  5. Mix.

  6. Add the coconut and rice flour and make a smooth dough.

  7. Line the sandwich tin with baking paper or grease it.

  8. Transfer the dough to the mold.

  9. Cut the last banana in half lengthwise and place on the surface of the dough.

  10. Press it lightly into the dough.

  11. Bake for about 60 minutes until the bread is baked and golden.

  12. Let cool before slicing.

Tip

This recipe can be easily adapted for allergen testing or training. Because it is baked for a long time, it tends to be well tolerated and allows for even distribution of the test ingredient across multiple servings. For example, when testing milk, butter or cow's milk can be used instead of dairy-free alternatives.
