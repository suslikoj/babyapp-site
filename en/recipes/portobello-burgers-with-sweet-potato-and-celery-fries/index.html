Portobello burgers with sweet potato and celery fries

Juicy burger sandwiched between two roasted portobello mushrooms instead of a bun. Served with avocado, fresh vegetables and sweet potato-celery fries.

Prep
25 min
Cook
30 min
Total
55 min
Servings
3
Difficulty
medium

Allergen-free

Contains allergens: none of the tracked allergens

May contain: Tomato, Root vegetables

Free from (by ingredients): dairy, egg, wheat, soy, nuts, peanuts, fish

Ingredients

for 3 servings

  • 6 pc Portobello mushroom
  • 600 g Minced meat (pork, lamb, turkey)
  • 1 pc Onion
  • 3 spoons Rice flour
  • 2 spoons Lard
  • 3 spoons Avomayo
  • 1 handful Cherry tomatoes – optional
  • 1 pc Red onion
  • 1 handful Fresh herbs
  • 3 handfuls Sweet potato and celery fries – optional

Method

  1. Burgers:

  2. Finely chop the onion.

  3. Mix it with minced meat, rice flour and salt.

  4. Shape into 3 larger burgers.

  5. Heat the lard in a pan and fry the burgers over low heat for approximately 6-8 minutes on each side, until cooked through.

  6. Portobello:

  7. Remove the stems from the mushrooms.

  8. Fry them in a pan for 5 minutes on each side, until soft and lightly golden.

  9. Assembly:

  10. Spread avomayo on one mushroom.

  11. Add the meat burger, tomatoes, onions and fresh herbs.

  12. Cover with the second mushroom.

  13. Serve with sweet potato and celery fries.

Tip

Celeriac contains less starch than potatoes and acquires a slightly nutty flavor when cooked. Combined with sweet potatoes, it creates a more balanced side dish with a higher fiber content and a wider range of vitamins and minerals.
