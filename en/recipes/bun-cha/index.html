Bún chả – pork flank with rice noodles and herbs

A lighter, homemade version of Vietnamese bún chả. Marinated pork belly adds full flavor and quality fats, rice noodles are naturally gluten-free, and fresh vegetables and herbs lighten the meal. The sweet and sour dressing supports digestion and balances the fat content of the meat.

Prep
25 min
Cook
45 min
Total
70 min
Servings
4
Difficulty
medium

Allergen-free

Contains allergens: none of the tracked allergens

Free from (by ingredients): dairy, egg, wheat, soy, nuts, peanuts, fish

Ingredients

for 4 servings

  • Meat:
  • 500 g Pork flank
  • 1 spoon Rice vinegar (či jablečný)
  • 1 spoon Coconut sugar
  • 1 teaspoon Salt
  • 2 cloves Grated garlic
  • 2 cm Grated ginger
  • 0.5 teaspoon Coriander
  • Noodles and vegetables:
  • 150 g Rice noodle
  • 1 pc Sweet potato – optional
  • 4 handfuls Salad
  • 2 pc Cucumber
  • 1 handful Herbs (mint, coriander)
  • Dressing:
  • 9 spoons Water
  • 3 teaspoons Apple cider vinegar
  • 1.5 spoon Sweetener (honey, maple syrup)
  • 1.5 teaspoon Grated ginger

Method

  1. Marinate the meat: Clean the meat and cut it into slices or larger cubes. In a bowl, mix the vinegar, sugar/syrup, garlic, ginger, salt and spices. Cover the meat in the marinade thoroughly and let it sit for at least 30 minutes, ideally overnight in the refrigerator.

  2. Meat preparation: Baking: Place the meat in a baking dish, cover and bake at 160°C for 45-60 minutes, then uncover and bake briefly until crispy. Or pan: Sear first in a dry pan, skin side down, then turn, add the marinade and caramelize.

  3. Noodles and vegetables: Prepare rice noodles according to package directions. Grate sweet potato, tear lettuce, slice cucumber, and chop herbs.

  4. Dressing: Mix all the ingredients for the dressing and taste – it should be slightly sweet and sour.

  5. Serving: Place noodles, vegetables, meat in bowls and pour dressing over. Serve immediately.

Tip

For a lighter version, use less meat and add more vegetables and herbs. The meat can be prepared in advance and reheated briefly the next day.
