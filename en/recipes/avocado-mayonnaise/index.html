Avocado mayonnaise

Creamy and fresh avocado dip with lemon juice. A great addition to roasted vegetables or meat.

Prep
5 min
Total
5 min
Servings
4
Difficulty
easy

VeganUnder 30 minNo cook

Contains allergens: none of the tracked allergens

May contain: Citrus

Free from (by ingredients): dairy, egg, wheat, soy, nuts, peanuts, fish

Ingredients

for 4 servings

  • 2 pcs Ripe avocado
  • 2 spoons Lemon juice

Method

  1. Cut the avocado, remove the pit and scoop the flesh into a bowl.

  2. Mash with a fork or blend until smooth.

  3. Add lemon juice and salt.

  4. Mix until creamy.

Tip

Lemon juice not only adds flavor, but also slows down the browning of the avocado.
