Heavenly white bean porridge

A delicate sweet porridge made from white beans that will surprise you with its creamy consistency and neutral taste. The beans provide high-quality plant protein and fiber, while spirulina or green superfoods support micronutrient intake. A great breakfast or snack without grains.

Prep
10 min
Cook
20 min
Total
30 min
Servings
2
Difficulty
easy

VeganOne potOvernightTravel friendly

Contains allergens: Legumes

May contain: Nuts, Exotic fruits

Free from (by ingredients): dairy, egg, wheat, soy, peanuts, fish

Ingredients

for 2 servings

  • 250 g White beans
  • 1 handful Raisins
  • 1 spoon Nut butter (can be replaced with coconut oil) – optional
  • 1 teaspoon Blue spirulina (či zelený ječmen nebo chlorella)
  • 2 handfuls Fruit

Method

  1. Soak the beans overnight in a sufficient amount of water.

  2. The next day, drain the water, cover the beans with clean water, and cook for approximately 20 minutes until they are soft.

  3. Drain the beans, transfer to a blender and add raisins, a cup of water and nut butter, which can be replaced with coconut oil or ghee.

  4. Add spirulina or a green superfood of your choice.

  5. Blend until smooth, creamy.

  6. Transfer to a bowl, garnish with fruit and serve.

Tip

You can make the porridge without spirulina – it will still taste very mild. If you are making it for children, start with a smaller amount of superfoods or leave them out altogether.
