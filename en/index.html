Is your baby troubled by eczema, colic or reflux and you don’t know what could be behind it?

Maybe you’re in one of these spots. Here’s how Baby Without Allergies helps.

  • Eczema, mucus in the stool, colic, reflux, restless nights – and you can’t tell if it’s food, teething, illness, the weather or your own stress?

    Log everything that might be connected in a moment, and the app helps you make sense of it.

    You get an elimination plan, day by day – what to skip and for how long.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the app designed for?

Baby Without Allergies is primarily designed for parents of babies and children with eczema, suspected food allergies, or a need to track an elimination diet and the gradual introduction of allergens.

Does the app replace medical advice or an examination?

No. The app is a practical guide and tracking tool, but it does not replace a medical examination, diagnosis, or individual treatment plan. Always discuss your child’s health with a pediatrician or allergist.

How can the app help me?

The app helps you track your child’s symptoms, diet, consumed allergens, and important events in one place. It also includes guidance for elimination diets and gradual allergen testing, as well as recipes and educational content.

Is the app available for Android and iPhone?

Yes. The app is available on Google Play for Android devices and on the App Store for iPhone. Download links are available directly on this website.

What is included in Premium?

Premium provides access to extended features, complete programs, and additional app content. The current features, subscription options, and price are always displayed in the app before you confirm a purchase.

What should I do if a reaction occurs during allergen testing?

Stop the test and follow the advice provided by your child’s healthcare professional. In the event of serious symptoms, such as difficulty breathing, swelling, or a significant deterioration, contact emergency medical services immediately.

How can I send a question or provide feedback?

Contact us through the website contact form or the email address shown on the website. Your feedback helps us continue improving the app.
