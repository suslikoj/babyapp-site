# **Yeast vs. Eczema**

## **Who is to blame?**

Yeast is often cited as the main culprit in eczema. However, **the problem is not really the yeast itself**, but rather **the yeast in an environment of disrupted skin and gut balance**.

Yeast (most commonly Candida) is **a normal part of the microbiome** – it lives on the skin, in the intestines and on mucous membranes. The problem arises when the microbiome is weakened or unbalanced. At that time, the yeast can start to behave more aggressively and **worsen inflammatory symptoms**, including eczema.

We know a similar principle in adult women - vaginal mycosis is not caused by the "arrival of yeast from outside", but by a disturbed environment in which the yeast multiplies.

### **Yeast in eczema ≠ a separate diagnosis**

It is important to distinguish:

- **eczema with the presence of yeast**,
- versus the notion that "eczema is caused by yeast."

**Yeast itself does not cause eczema.**  
But **inflamed, broken skin** is an ideal environment where yeast can more easily take hold and impair healing.

That's why we often see:

- worsening of eczema in skin folds,
- redness that does not respond to normal care,
- repeated returns of difficulties.

### **Why Extreme “Anti-Yeast” Diets Don’t Work**

A common reaction is to try to "starve" the yeast - **a strict carbohydrate-free or very restrictive diet.** However, this can do **more harm than good**.

In women (especially breastfeeding women) it can lead to:

- exhaustion of the body,
- hormonal imbalance,
- loss or reduction of breastfeeding,
- further weakening of the microbiome.

For children, then:

- unbalanced energy intake,
- microbiome depletion,
- slowing down the regeneration of the skin and intestines.

The microbiome **is not restored by starvation**, but **by** **balance**.

**What really makes sense**

The basic step is **not to eliminate all carbohydrates**, but:

**1. Eliminate simple sugars**

- sweets,
- sweetened drinks,
- white sugar,
- industrially processed foods.

**2. Maintain complex carbohydrates**

- vegetables,
- root vegetables,
- quality cereals according to tolerance,
- legumes.

**3. Support the microbiome**

- varied, real food,
- plenty of fiber,
- **green vegetables** (broccoli, zucchini, leafy greens),
- quality fats.

**4. Address the whole, not one “culprit”**

Yeast is a **signal**, not a cause. If the gut, diet, inflammation, and environment are not addressed, it will return.

**Summary**

- Yeast is a normal part of the body – the problem arises when **the microbiome is imbalanced.**
- Eczema is not a "yeast as a disease," but rather **a yeast in an inflamed environment.**
- Extreme diets can be harmful, especially for breastfeeding women and young children.
- The key is to **eliminate simple sugar**, not all carbohydrates.
- A real, varied diet and gut support are key to long-term improvement.
